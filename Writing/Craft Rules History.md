# Craft Rules History

Why each rule in `Craft Rules.md` exists. Don't load this during play; open it when adding or changing a rule, to see whether the pattern has come up before.

## Where the rules came from

- **§3 to §9** were carried over from the Living World Engine's `House Rules/Craft Rules.md` when this engine was built (2026-10-07), reworded for third person limited and for beats. The reasons behind them are in that engine's `Craft Rules History.md`. Rules that only made sense for chapters, outlines, or the audit scripts were left behind.
- **§1 (The game stays off the page)** and **§2 (The player's character)** were written for this engine from what the user asked of it: no numbers in the prose, and a GM who never plays the user's character.
- A few rules were added for play that the novels never needed: "People don't hand over what the character came for," "Nobody exists for the character," "Names come from the pools," "A beat is as long as what happened," "Beats join into one continuous narrative," "No endings on a beat," "Time passes in narration."
