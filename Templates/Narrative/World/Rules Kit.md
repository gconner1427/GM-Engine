# Rules Kit: {{NARRATIVE}}

This world's layer on the engine's Core Rules. The core is the same in every world (six attributes, one kind of roll, five outcomes). The kit names what this world puts on top of it: its skills, its gear, its powers, its talents. Built during setup (Procedures/New Narrative.md) and added to as play needs it.

## Skills

Twelve to twenty skills that cover what people do in this world, each with a line on what it covers. Every kit needs something for: fighting up close, fighting at range, moving and climbing, going unseen, noticing things, getting by in the wild or the street, knowing things, making and fixing, treating the hurt, winning people over, lying, and leaning on people. Add what the world adds (riding, piloting, sorcery, hacking).

## Powers

One entry for each law in the World Bible that a person can use (a school of magic, psionics, implants, a mutation). The World Bible says what it is in the world; this says how it rolls.

```
### Name of the power
**Skill:** the skill that drives it
**Can do:** what's in its reach, in words
**Can't do:** its hard limits
**Cost:** what each use takes (Strain by size, a material, time, something worse)
**When it goes wrong:** what a Partial costs and what a Disaster does
```
