# Items

Notable items: things that matter across scenes, change hands, or could turn one (a letter, a key, a named blade, a data chip, a stash). Everyday things stay on **Has** lines and in a place's **Has** section. Read only the entry for an item in the scene.

Every item has exactly one **Holder**: the player character, someone in the cast, a place, a group, or "Archive: <final fate>" once it's out of play.

Entry format:

```
### Name of the item
**Also called:** other names people use for it
**Holder:** who or where has it now
**Where:** exactly where it is (in his coat's inside pocket, under the loose board)
**State:** its condition (cracked, half full, unloaded)
**Rules:** anything it does in the rules (damage size, armor, a power), if it does anything
**Hidden:** what's true of it that the character doesn't know, if anything
- History, one line per change of hands or state, with tags.
```
