# World Bible: {{NARRATIVE}}

How this world works. You built it from the player's premise (Procedures/New Narrative.md), and you obey it every time (Craft Rules, "World laws hold"). The player has never read it: they learn the world only by living in it. What their character knows is in `Journal.md`.

Every fact here is one of two kinds, and the difference matters every time someone speaks:
- **Common knowledge:** what an ordinary person in the region knows or believes. Not always true.
- **Hidden:** what's actually so, and who knows it.
