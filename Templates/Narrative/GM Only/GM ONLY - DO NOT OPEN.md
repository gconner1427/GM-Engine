# GM only

Everything in this folder belongs to the GM: the world itself and how it really works, what other people want and know, what's moving where your character can't see, every roll, and every ruling. Reading it spoils the narrative the same way reading the last chapter does.

Nothing stops you from opening it. It's an honor system.

What your character knows is in `Journal.md`, and their numbers are on `Character Sheet.md`. If you want to know something, ask the GM in braces.
