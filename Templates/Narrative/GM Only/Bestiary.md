# Bestiary

Stat lines for the kinds of things in this world that can hurt a person and aren't individuals with a cast doc: beasts, monsters, machines, and common sorts of people (a town guard, a raider). Write an entry the first time one is needed, so the next one of its kind is the same. Read only the entry in the scene.

Entry format (Core Rules, "Everyone else"):

```
### Name of the kind
**Tier:** 2
**Stat line:** +4 · Guard 13 · Toughness 13 · Resolve 13 · HP 14 · Damage d8+1 · Armor 0
**Strong at:** what it gets +2 on
**Weak at:** what it gets -2 on
**How it fights:** what it wants out of a fight, what it does first, and when it runs or gives up
**Traits:** anything it can do that a person can't, in words
```
