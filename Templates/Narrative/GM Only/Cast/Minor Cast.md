# Minor Cast

Everyone the narrative has met or named who doesn't need a full doc: one `###` entry each. Read only the entry for someone in the scene. When someone starts to matter (they come back, they want something from the character, they carry a secret), give them a full doc with `gm add cast "<Name>"` and move their lines into it.

Entry format:

```
### Name
**Also called:** what people call them
**Who:** what they are and what they look like, in a line
**Wants:** what they're after, in general or right now
**Voice:** how they talk, in a line
**Stat line:** only if they've needed one (Core Rules, "Everyone else")
**Where:** where they are or can be found
- What the page has established about them, one line each, tagged.
```
