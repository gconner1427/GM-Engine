The engine

About

GM Engine is a game master for solo role-playing. You play one character; the GM plays everyone and everything else, and writes what happens as a story.

How you play

You say what kind of story you want, and you make one character. From then on you say only what that character does. The GM answers with what happens next, in prose that reads like a novel: no hit points, no damage numbers, and no dice rolls on the page.

The world is the GM's

The GM builds the world from your premise and keeps it to itself, the way a game master prepares a campaign. You learn its places, its people and its secrets the way your character does, by going and finding out. Nothing in this window shows you the GM's notes.

A real game underneath

Behind the story there are rules and real dice. When something is uncertain, the GM rolls for it out of sight, and the result stands, whether or not it goes your way. Your character has health, skills and gear, earns levels, and can fail, be hurt, or die. Other people want things of their own, and the world keeps moving while you are elsewhere.

What you get

  • The story, one session at a time, kept to read back.
  • A character sheet and inventory that change as you play.
  • A journal of what your character knows about people, places and unfinished business.
  • Pictures of the major moments: your portrait, a place that matters, a new face. They show only what your character has seen.
  • Saved games, kept on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.

What runs it

The GM is Claude, run through Claude Code with your own sign-in. Pictures are painted by Codex, if you have it installed and signed in. Each narrative lives in a folder of its own, and you can run as many as you like.
